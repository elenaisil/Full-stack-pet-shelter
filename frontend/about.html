About Our Shelter

Who we are and what we believe in

🐾 Our Story

We are four passionate entrepreneurs who share one common love - animals. Isil, Vatey, Meriem, and Dana came together with a mission to make a difference in the lives of homeless pets.

What started as a small dream has grown into ADOPT Pet Shelter, where every animal that comes through our doors receives love, care, and a second chance at happiness.

We believe that every pet deserves a loving home, and we are committed to make it a reality for as many animals as possible - one adoption at a time.

Our Mission

We are proud to say that we have successfully found loving homes for hundreds of animals. Every adoption fills our hearts with joy and motivates us to work even harder.

Our mission is simple: to find every animal a safe, loving, and permanent home. We don't stop until each pet in our care finds their perfect family.

We are proud of our work, proud of our team, and most importantly, proud of every family who chooses to adopt.
